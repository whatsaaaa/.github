# Support

Questions go to the issues of the repository they are about. Search the open and closed issues first: the answer may already be there.

- Something seems broken: use the **Bug report** form.
- You want something new, or you are not sure how to do something: use the **Feature request** form.
- A security problem: do not open an issue, follow the [security policy](https://github.com/whatsaaaa/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
